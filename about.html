Who we are

Structuring Climate Finance For Global Impact

Equiterra Capital structures climate finance solutions and environmental markets for governments, financial institutions, and corporations. We operate at the intersection of regulatory intelligence, carbon markets, and financial structuring to transform sustainability commitments into scalable and investable outcomes.

Equiterra exists to close the distance between climate ambition and deployable capital, combining regulatory depth, market structuring and on-the-ground delivery.

Our Vision

To integrate environmental value into financial systems with the same rigor as traditional assets, enabling scalable and investable climate solutions. We aim to bridge sustainability and capital markets, transforming how environmental impact is valued, financed, and deployed globally.

Through this approach, we support the transition toward a more resilient, low-carbon economy.

Alexis Ludwig Leroy
Founder & Principal, Equiterra

Founder of ALLCOT and Equiterra Capital, is a climate finance entrepreneur and carbon market specialist with over two decades of experience in environmental markets, sustainable finance, and international project development.

His work focuses on structuring credible environmental assets and mobilizing climate capital across global markets. With expertise in carbon market architecture, Article 6 mechanisms, and nature-based solutions, he supports governments, financial institutions, and partners in turning sustainability commitments into investable, scalable climate solutions.

An active contributor to international climate discussions, he collaborates with leading organizations and remains engaged in global policy forums shaping the future of climate finance.
