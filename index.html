Our methodology

The CM4GE Framework

CM4GE — Carbon Markets for Global Equity — is our framework for environmental markets and climate finance. It ensures projects have strong integrity, aligned incentives, and long-term impact.

1

Market and regulatory integrity

Additionality and permanence built into the asset, not bolted on.

2

Equitable benefit-sharing

Communities and stewards participate in the value created.

3

Long-term environmental value

Durable outcomes that hold beyond the crediting period.

4

Institutional capacity

Clear roles, data and accountability across every counterparty.

5

Cross-jurisdictional compliance

Structured to meet Article 6, CBAM and buyer requirements, with MRV that turns outcomes into verifiable, financeable results.
