Privacy Policy

What information we collect through this website, why, and how you can control it.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Controller identity

This website is operated by Equiterra Capital LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (EIN 41-4264040). For data protection purposes, Equiterra Capital LLC acts as the data controller of personal data collected through this website. Contact: info@equiterra.capital.

2. Scope and applicable law

This Policy applies to personal data collected through this website. Equiterra Capital LLC operates internationally, including from Colombia. Depending on your jurisdiction, the following frameworks may apply:

  • EU/EEA residents: General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • Colombian residents: Ley 1581 de 2012 and Decreto 1377 de 2013
  • Other jurisdictions: applicable local data protection law

3. Data we collect

  • Contact data — name, email address, organization — when voluntarily submitted.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information — collected automatically.
  • Usage data — pages visited, interaction patterns.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data.

5. How we use your data

  • To respond to inquiries
  • To provide information about our services
  • To maintain and improve website functionality
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

6. Data sharing

We may share personal data with:

  • Website hosting and analytics providers (currently operating in the United States)
  • Professional advisors, where necessary and under confidentiality obligations
  • Competent authorities, where required by applicable law

7. International data transfers

Personal data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other jurisdictions. For EU/EEA residents, transfers are conducted under standard contractual clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission. For Colombian residents, transfers outside Colombia are conducted in accordance with Ley 1581 and applicable SIC requirements.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data according to the following schedule:

  • Contact inquiries (no ongoing relationship): 2 years from last interaction
  • Counterparty, client, or commercial contact data: 7 years from end of relationship
  • Website analytics and technical data: up to 26 months
  • Legal and compliance records: duration of applicable obligation plus 1 year

Data is deleted or anonymized once the applicable retention period expires.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, or to withdraw consent. Submit requests to info@equiterra.capital. We respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 10 business days for consultation requests (Ley 1581).

10. Cookies

This website uses cookies for analytics and functionality. Where required by applicable law, cookies are only placed with your prior consent. You may manage or withdraw consent at any time through the cookie preference tool on this website or through your browser settings.

11. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

12. Third-party links

This website may link to third-party sites. Equiterra Capital LLC is not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy periodically. The revision date appears at the top of this page.

14. Contact and complaints

For questions or to exercise your rights: info@equiterra.capital. EU residents may lodge complaints with their local supervisory authority. Colombian residents may contact the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC): www.sic.gov.co.
