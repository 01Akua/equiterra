Privacy Policy

What information we collect through this website, why, and how you can control it.

Last updated: 28 July 2026

1. Who we are

This policy applies to equiterra.capital, operated by Equiterra Capital ("Equiterra", "we", "us"). It explains what personal information we collect through the site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact form submissions — name, email, organization, and any message you submit through the "Partner With Us" or project inquiry forms.
  • Automatically collected data — standard web log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring URL) collected via our hosting and analytics tools.
  • Cookies and local storage — used to remember your language preference and, where enabled, for basic analytics.

3. How we use this information

  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our contact and partnership forms.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the website (e.g. understanding which pages are useful).
  • To communicate with prospective partners, investors and counterparties who have opted in by contacting us.

We do not sell personal information to third parties, and we do not use form submissions for purposes other than those stated above without your consent.

4. Sharing of information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate the site (e.g. hosting, email delivery) under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law. We do not share your information with unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain our business records, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, or to object to certain uses. To exercise these rights, contact us at inquiries@equiterra.capital.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information submitted through this site. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to inquiries@equiterra.capital.
