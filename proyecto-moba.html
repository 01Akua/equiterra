The project

Restoring degraded miombo savanna in the Tanganyika corridor.

The Congo Basin holds the world's largest tropical carbon reservoir. Equiterra is structuring an ARR and agroforestry project across 10,000 hectares in Kayabala, Kansabala and Manda chefferies, Moba Territory — sitting within DRC's priority restoration belt under the 2021 NDC, paired with a community permaculture component.

What we're building

ARR and permaculture, side by side

Mixed-species ARR paired with community permaculture school-farms producing fertilizer, fruit, fuel and medicinal trees — reducing slash-and-burn pressure while native-species planting progressively replaces initial nursery stock.

  • Sovereign non-objection, Ministry of Environment — May 2024
  • 40-year community partnership signed August 2025
  • Gold Standard listing targeted, 30-year crediting period
  • Women-led producer associations since 2022
Project timeline

From community engagement, to first carbon issuance

2022–2024

Project foundations

Community engagement begins with Graine de Vie. Pilot planting of 1,000 ha (June 2023). Protocol of Collaboration signed with DRC's Ministry of Environment. Sovereign non-objection issued and revised.

2025

Community partnership

40-year Community Partnership Contract signed in August 2025, allocating a substantial share of net carbon benefits to the community.

2026–2027

Structuring

Gold Standard listing targeted; validation and registration to follow.

2028

Toward first issuance

First issuance targeted under the 30-year crediting period.
