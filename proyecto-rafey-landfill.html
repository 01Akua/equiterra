The project

Turning a 40-year-old liability into a climate asset.

The Rafey landfill has received waste for over 40 years — 679,000 tons in 2025 alone, across 13 municipalities. Equiterra is structuring the capture and transportation of landfill gas to neighboring industrial facilities in Santiago de los Caballeros, displacing imported fossil fuels for industrial heat and process fuel.

A circular economy project

Waste as a resource, not a liability

Captured biogas displaces imported fossil fuels across industrial heat, process fuel and potentially vehicle fuel — closing the loop at both the material level (landfill mass to energy) and the energy-system level (recovered gas to fossil-fuel displacement).

  • Methodology A6.4-AMM-001 — flaring or use of landfill gas
  • Aligned with DR Law 225-20 and the PLANGIR national roadmap
  • Active conversations for Corresponding Adjustment under Article 6.4
  • Local partner: Cilpen Global
