The project

Restoring one of the planet's most irreplaceable ecosystems.

The Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve and Colombia's highest coastal mountain range — home to indigenous peoples who have governed it under their own legal and spiritual systems for centuries. Within the Río Ariguaní watershed, Equiterra is structuring an early-stage ARR project restoring ~5,000 hectares of degraded forest across 90 collectively-titled predios, targeting at least 6 tCO₂e per hectare (net), with Saumama Foundation as community-side counsel.

Community at the core

Built with the community, not around them

This project is co-designed with local indigenous authorities in accordance with their traditional legal and territorial governance. Saumama Foundation accompanies the communities as neutral legal and social adviser, and carbon revenue flows back through the governance of the 9 participating communities.

  • Free, prior and informed consent (FPIC)
  • Validation Agreement signed with Equiterra Capital and Saumama Foundation
  • Ancestral knowledge guiding restoration practices
  • 40-year community association agreement with the 9 communities
