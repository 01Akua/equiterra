Our approach

Structured Approaches to Environmental Finance

Equiterra Capital develops structured solutions that translate environmental value into credible financial and institutional frameworks. Our work spans advisory, project development, environmental markets and climate finance structuring.

Equiterra Capital operates not only as an advisor but also as a structuring partner in the development of environmental assets and climate finance mechanisms.

01, Advisory

Advisory & Strategy

Strategic guidance for institutions seeking to integrate sustainability frameworks into core operations, regulatory positioning and capital allocation.

  • Climate strategy development and institutional roadmaps
  • Net-zero pathway design and target-setting frameworks
  • Environmental risk assessment and mitigation strategies
  • Sustainability governance and ESG integration

02, Development

Project Development

End-to-end project origination, structuring, and implementation across environmental and sustainable infrastructure sectors.

  • Nature-based solutions (reforestation, conservation, ecosystem restoration)
  • Waste management and circular economy solutions
  • Renewable energy and clean infrastructure projects
  • Sustainable land-use and climate-smart agriculture initiatives
  • Blue carbon and coastal ecosystem restoration

03, Markets

Environmental Markets

Technical and strategic expertise across carbon markets, biodiversity credits and emerging environmental asset classes.

  • Carbon credit project structuring and certification
  • Paris Agreement Article 6 implementation and ITMO development
  • Voluntary carbon market participation and transaction support
  • Biodiversity and nature credit mechanisms

04, Finance

Impact Finance Structuring

Financial engineering and capital mobilization for sustainability-linked investments and environmental assets.

  • Blended finance structures and concessional capital coordination
  • Green bonds and sustainability-linked financial instruments
  • Climate fund design and investment facilitation
  • Results-based financing and performance-based payment structures
05, Sovereign

Sovereign & Institutional Advisory

Strategic advisory to governments, multilateral institutions, and public sector entities on climate policy and environmental market development.

  • Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC) implementation strategies
  • National carbon market design and institutional frameworks
  • International climate cooperation and bilateral agreements
  • Access to climate finance and multilateral funding mechanisms
06, Compliance

CBAM & Trade-Related Compliance

Specialized advisory on EU climate and environmental regulations affecting international trade, including the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), the EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS), and the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR).

  • CBAM reporting requirements and compliance architecture
  • Embedded emissions calculation and verification
  • Supply chain decarbonization and trade impact mitigation
  • EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) compliance frameworks
  • Regulatory intelligence and policy monitoring
Learn more →
EU Regulatory Expertise

EU Regulatory Expertise

Equiterra Capital provides targeted support for institutions and companies navigating EU climate regulations, including CBAM, EU ETS, CSRD, and EUDR.

Advisory related to CBAM and EU ETS is delivered in partnership with MiFID-regulated financial entities, combining specialized regulatory expertise with Equiterra's experience in environmental markets and climate finance structuring.

Our approach translates regulatory complexity into practical compliance and strategic positioning.

07, Capacity

Capacity Building & Knowledge Transfer

Technical training and institutional strengthening programs designed to build durable sustainability and climate finance capabilities.

  • Carbon market fundamentals and project development training
  • Climate finance structuring and investment evaluation
  • Regulatory compliance and monitoring systems
  • Institutional frameworks for environmental market participation
